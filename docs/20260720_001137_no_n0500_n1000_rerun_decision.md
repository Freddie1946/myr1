## Required limitation

The fixed-epoch scale table is pragmatic rather than a perfectly controlled backend ablation:
n=500/n=1000 used the earlier ZeRO-3 optimizer-offload implementation, whereas n=2000/n=3000 used
the later ZeRO-2 GPU fused-AdamW implementation. All runs used the same base revision, data subsets,
seed, learning rate, 10 epochs, full-language training and frozen vision/projector policy, and all
formal gates passed. Nevertheless, numerical/backend differences must be disclosed, and the paper
must not claim that sample count alone causally explains every difference.

The old n=500/n=1000 final models, two resume checkpoints per run, manifests, logs, tensor gates and
385-row raw validation predictions remain preserved. Their predictions re-score with zero mismatches
under the current parser.
